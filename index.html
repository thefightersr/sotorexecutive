FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

How do I book a ride?

Fill in the request form or message us directly on WhatsApp. You will receive a confirmation with a fixed price.

How much is a transfer from Zurich Airport?

The price depends on route, vehicle and time. Send us your pickup and destination and you will receive a binding quote in advance.

What if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight and adjust the pickup time to your actual landing.

Do you offer corporate invoicing and B2B partnerships?

Yes. Companies, hotels, travel agencies and DMCs get corporate invoicing and a dedicated point of contact.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, on request. Please include the child's age in your request.
